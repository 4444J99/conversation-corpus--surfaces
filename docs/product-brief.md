# Product brief

Conversation Corpus Engine (CCE) turns AI chat exports into one local,
validated, searchable corpus. This repo is its ORGAN-II surfaces bridge: it
packages that capability for people and tools without pulling billing into the
engine.

## Audience

Builders, researchers, consultants, and small teams whose AI conversations
contain decisions worth keeping.

## H1 wedge

- Installable MCP server (`cce[mcp]`) for tool hosts.
- Public landing page with waitlist capture.
- Public launch copy (`docs/hn-post.md`).
- Stable boundary contract (`packages/interface-contracts/`).

## Value proposition

Scattered provider exports become a governed corpus with freshness checks,
validation records, federation, candidate diffs, and cited retrieval — all
local-first.

## Non-goals

Stripe, pricing, licenses, seats, and invoice state belong to the ORGAN-III
product repo. Raw provider export custody and corpus mutation belong to the
ORGAN-I engine.
